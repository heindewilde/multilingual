We built Tidynote because every note app we tried made us choose between speed and structure.

## The problem

Plain text apps are fast but turn into a pile within 3 months. Database-style apps keep things tidy but take 10 clicks to write a single line.

## What we left out

- No AI rewriting of your notes
- No social feed
- No [complicated pricing](/pricing)

## What comes next

We ship a small update every 2 weeks. Follow along on the blog.
