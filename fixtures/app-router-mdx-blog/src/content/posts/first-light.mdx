The best light of the day lasts about 30 minutes, and most people sleep through it.

## Why mornings beat evenings

Evening light is just as warm, but the streets are full. At 6 in the morning you have the city to yourself.

## How to plan it

1. Check the sunrise time the night before.
2. Arrive 20 minutes early to find your spot.
3. Shoot until the shadows get hard, then stop.

> The alarm is the hardest part. Everything after it is easy.
